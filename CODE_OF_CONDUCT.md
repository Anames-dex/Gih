# Gih Community Code of Conduct

As maintainers, contributors, and users of the Gih project, we are committed to fostering a welcoming, respectful, and collaborative environment for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

---

## 1. Our Standards

We expect all members of the community to demonstrate constructive and thoughtful behavior:

- **Respect and Kindness**: Treat fellow contributors and users with dignity, patience, and empathy.
- **Constructive Communication**: Focus on constructive feedback, technical substance, and solving problems collaboratively.
- **Openness to Perspective**: Welcome diverse points of view, and gracefully accept constructive criticism.
- **Focus on the Community**: Prioritize what is best for the overall project, its users, and its code health.

Behaviors that are unacceptable include:
- Harassing, demeaning, derogatory, or exclusionary language and conduct.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or insulting comments directed at individuals or groups.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Disruptive conduct in project issue trackers, pull requests, or communication channels.

---

## 2. Responsibilities of Maintainers

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, pull requests, and issues that violate this Code of Conduct.

---

## 3. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including the GitHub repository, issue trackers, pull requests, discussions, and any official communication channels associated with Gih.

---

## 4. Reporting and Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project team directly at:

**natelligence.ai@gmail.com**

All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion. Project maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporting party.

---

## 5. Attribution

These guidelines are tailored specifically for the Gih open-source ecosystem, reflecting our commitment to civil, technical, and respectful collaboration.
