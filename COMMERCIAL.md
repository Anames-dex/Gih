# Commercial Licensing & Team Trials for Gih

Gih is **source-available** software licensed under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE).

## Free Use
Gih is **free of charge** for:
- Personal and individual use
- Educational, academic, and student use
- Open-source project contributions
- Non-profit organizations
- Small teams and businesses with **10 or fewer users**

## Commercial Teams (> 10 Users)
Companies and organizations with **more than 10 users** require a commercial license after an evaluation period:
- **Free Trial**: **14-day (2-week) free trial** so your team can evaluate Gih in your real workflow.
- **Pricing**: **$5 USD per user / month** (or **$50 USD per user / year**).
